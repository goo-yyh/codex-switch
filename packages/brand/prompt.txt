Use case: logo-brand / stylized-concept.
Create ONE distinctive macOS app icon for a developer tool that turns model routing on and off.
Visual metaphor must be immediately concrete and recognizable: a beautiful physical toggle switch, NOT an abstract letter or arrow symbol.

On the FACE of the white circular knob, an engraved-looking deep indigo generic terminal prompt ">_" is large enough to be clearly legible. Premium crafted appearance, cohesive and memorable.
Palette: bright clean blue #4D7CFA at left transitioning to periwinkle #7967EB at right; warm-white tile and knob. Avoid charcoal, black tile, muddy gray, green, red and rainbow.
Output: single centered square 1024px app icon. The white tile must be solid opaque; only the area OUTSIDE its rounded-square silhouette is transparent. Keep exterior edge smooth with no halo, stray pixels or specks. No official product logo, no flowers, rosettes, knots, existing logo silhouettes, arrows, captions, wordmarks, letters S or C, watermark, mockup sheet, multiple icons, or surrounding desktop.
